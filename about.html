Anthony DeCesare

Principal Adjuster, Thoroughbred Unlimited, Inc.

Background

Four decades of large-loss adjusting.

Anthony has been a licensed insurance adjuster since the early 1980s, starting his career in the independent adjusting insurance business and building a solid background as both an independent and a company staff adjuster.

That career led to the handling of a variety of high-profile, multi-million-dollar insurance claims throughout the United States — commercial property, business income and extra expense, inland marine and builders risk, agriculture and food, industrial and specialty equipment, and nationwide catastrophe response.

He has worked on notable assignments such as the Exxon Valdez oil spill on behalf of the Trans-Alaska Pipeline Liability Fund in Alaska, under the direction of the U.S. Federal Court. As an independent adjuster he has also served as an appointed appraiser and umpire.

Thoroughbred Unlimited, Inc. was founded to bring that level of handling directly to carriers, program managers, self-insureds and public entities — without a file being handed down a bench.

Professional Accomplishments

Appointments, instruction and industry service

Executive General Adjuster — Alaska

Worked exclusively as an executive general adjuster in Anchorage, Alaska (1990–1991) for the Trans-Alaska Pipeline Liability Fund, adjusting property damage and environmental business interruption losses resulting from the Exxon Valdez oil spill.

Appraiser & Umpire

As an independent adjuster, served as an appointed appraiser and umpire in disputed property valuations, delivering an independent determination of the amount of loss.

PLRB Course Instructor

Instructor of a PLRB online continuing-education accredited distance-learning module video course, Business Interruption and Extra Expense for Service Insureds.

Conference Presenter

Presenter of the Business Income & Extra Expense course at the PLRB Minneapolis Conference, September 2014, on business interruption and extra expense for service insureds.

Inland Marine & Claim Strategy

Presenter at PLRB Regional Conferences in Dallas, Texas; Columbus, Ohio; and Atlanta, Georgia on inland marine coverages and claim strategies.

Large Loss & Mega Claims

Presenter at PLRB Large Loss Regional Conferences on arson and fraud investigations (Washington, D.C., 2015) and Anatomy of a Mega Claim — Too Large to Fail (Nashville, TN, 2018), and at LEA on Challenges of a Mega Loss (Ft. Lauderdale, FL, 2019).
