Case Files

Selected assignments in detail

Cold Storage & Distribution Center — Total Loss Fire

Delanco, New Jersey · 265,000 SF · $210,000,000

A roof-mounted photovoltaic panel fire spread through a 265,000 square foot cold storage and distribution center, resulting in a total loss of the structure, the refrigeration systems and the stored product. The assignment combined building and equipment valuation, perishable product loss, contingent exposure for the tenants served by the facility, and the origin-and-cause work that a rooftop solar installation invites.

School District Hurricane Loss

Houma, Louisiana · Hurricane Ida · $255,000,000

Wind and water damage across an entire parish school district: roof systems, mechanical equipment, building envelopes, gymnasiums, auditoriums and contents, with the extra expense of keeping a district operating while its campuses were being rebuilt. A scoping standard consistent across dozens of buildings was the difference between a defensible number and a disputed one.

Furniture Manufacturing Facility — Total Loss Fire

North Carolina · $78,000,000

A total loss fire consumed a multi-building furniture manufacturing campus. The assignment covered the buildings, the production line and tooling, raw and finished goods inventory, and a business income measurement complicated by long equipment lead times and a period of restoration that ran well beyond the initial estimate.

Trans-Alaska Pipeline Liability Fund — Exxon Valdez

Anchorage, Alaska · 1990–1991 · $100,000,000

Assigned exclusively as executive general adjuster in Anchorage for the Trans-Alaska Pipeline Liability Fund, adjusting property damage and environmental business interruption losses arising from the Exxon Valdez oil spill, under the direction of the U.S. Federal Court. Pipeline break assignments involving business income, property damage and pollution followed.
