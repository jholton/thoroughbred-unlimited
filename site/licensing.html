Certificates

Federal and specialty certification

National Flood Insurance Program

Certificate holder, FCN 70004753 — residential, manufactured, small commercial, large commercial and condominium (RCBAP).

California Earthquake Certified

Certified for California earthquake loss assessment, including deductible application and ordinance and law exposure.

FAA Private Pilot

Federal Aviation Administration private pilot airman's certificate — an asset in reaching remote and post-catastrophe locations.

Deployment

Licensed where the storms land.

Gulf and Atlantic hurricane states, the tornado corridor, the California seismic zone and the flood program — covered before the event, not after it.

Emergency and non-resident licensing in additional states can generally be obtained within the deployment window. If an assignment falls outside the list above, call and ask.
